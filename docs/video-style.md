# PercentURL explainer video style

Use this system for every article explainer and its matching article diagrams.

## Visual system

- Canvas: 16:9 landscape, 1280×720; keep text and diagrams inside a 72 px safe margin.
- Background: warm white `#FBFCFE`; primary ink/navy `#123B63`; teal `#07878A`; amber `#F0A51A`; pale blue `#EAF3FA`; muted gray `#687789`.
- Use a clean sans serif for labels and a monospaced face for URL/code examples. Keep diagrams flat, spacious, and readable at phone size.
- One visual point per shot. Animate by moving between clean diagram states; do not decorate with unrelated stock footage or generated text inside artwork.
- Keep exact URL strings as typeset text. Verify every character against the article and example before export.
- Use consistent top-left PercentURL wordmark, numbered chapter cue, bottom caption band, and quiet end card linking to the matching guide.

## Editorial system

- One question and one central claim per video. Typical duration: 45–65 seconds, about 100–135 spoken words.
- Narration: clear, measured US English; explain symbols in plain words once, then show the exact notation on screen.
- Structure: question/hook → core rule → two or three visual steps → practical warning → concise takeaway and guide URL.
- Captions use sentence case, high contrast, no more than two lines at once, and are supplied as an SRT. Burn captions into the video for silent autoplay too.
- Keep technical distinctions precise. Do not imply percent-encoding is encryption, that all plus signs mean spaces, or that `encodeURI` and `encodeURIComponent` are interchangeable.

## YouTube packaging

- Title describes the actual question or distinction; description links to the exact matching article first.
- Use a consistent thumbnail: white field, dark navy headline, one large code/URL example, teal and amber accents, small PercentURL wordmark.
- Use the same clean frame rate, audio loudness, and caption typography across the series. Review text accuracy, mobile legibility, playback, and destination link before publishing.
