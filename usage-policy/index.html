Usage Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Free to use, with no sign-up, usage cap, or output watermark. PercentURL tools process the text you enter in your browser. The input is not uploaded to PercentURL for encoding or decoding.

Permitted use

You may use the tools for personal or business tasks, including preparing URL values, decoding percent-encoded text, and building campaign links. Use the tools only in ways that comply with applicable laws and respect other people’s rights.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for the text and URLs you submit, how you use the results, and checking that the output is suitable for your purpose. Percent-encoding changes how characters are represented; it is not encryption and does not protect confidential information.

Fair use

Please do not use the site to interfere with its operation, overload it with automated requests, or attempt to bypass security controls. We may limit access temporarily when needed to protect the service or other visitors.

Accuracy and availability

Encoding results depend on the mode and options you select. Review the result before using it in a production link or application. We may update the tools or their availability over time.

Privacy

The tools run locally in your browser and do not send the text you process to our servers. See the Privacy Policy for more about site privacy.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the Contact page.
