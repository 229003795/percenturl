Your text never leaves your browser. All URL encoding and decoding on PercentURL happens locally in your web browser using JavaScript. The text you enter is not uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted to any server.

What we do not collect

  • We do not receive or log the content you type into the tool.
  • We do not require an account, so we hold no user credentials.
  • We do not set tracking cookies or use analytics that identify you.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Advertising

We may display third-party advertisements on this site in the future. If and when we do, those third parties may use cookies or similar identifiers subject to their own privacy policies. We will not share the text you process with any advertiser. Currently, no advertisements are shown.

Links to other sites

This site is self-contained. If we ever link to external resources, those sites have their own privacy practices that this policy does not cover.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us through the Contact page.
